Little Crab Eddy-Current Leveling — Beginner Tutorial

 

Leveling uses I2C communication, so the wiring must not be too long. Use pins wired directly to the MCU — endstop / thermistor / fan pins cannot be used. The coil can be connected to SPI / I2C / UART pins or some spare stepper-driver pins.

Coil mounting height: the underside of the coil should be about 2 mm from the nozzle.
